== METHODOLOGY & CORRECTIONS ==

> DATA SOURCES

Every rate, term, fee, and track-record figure on this site comes from a named source, shown on the offer’s card with the date it was checked. We use, in order of preference:

  • The supplier’s own website and published contract summary for that supplier’s rate, term, early-termination fee, monthly fee, and rate type, checked for a Rhode Island ZIP code.
  • Empower RI / RI Public Utilities Commission — the state’s official comparison tool (RI PUC) — for listings of competitive offers in Rhode Island Energy territory.
  • RI PUC tariffs and filings for the Rhode Island Energy Last Resort (default) rate and for the state bill credits (for example, Tariff R.I.P.U.C. No. 2096 for Last Resort Service; No. 2304 and No. 2309 for the winter credits).
  • Third-party aggregator pages only where a supplier’s own Rhode Island terms could not be confirmed. Figures from these sources are always labeled ADVERTISED (see below) and are never presented as quotes.
  • Municipal program announcements from the towns themselves for municipal aggregation rates.

We never guess a fee, term, or rate. Where a source does not publish a field, the card reads “not confirmed.”

> VERIFICATION CADENCE

Rates are re-checked: (1) at each Last Resort rate reset (April 1 and October 1); (2) when a supplier, a town, or a visitor reports a change; and (3) on a periodic review cycle between resets. Every card carries its own “checked” date. A rate is an estimate as of its checked date — suppliers can change offers at any time, and the supplier’s own contract summary controls if it differs from anything shown here.

> HOW VERIFICATION LEVELS ARE LABELED

  • VERIFIED — the rate (and, where stated, the term/fees) was confirmed on the supplier’s own site or on Empower RI for a Rhode Island ZIP code on the checked date.
  • ADVERTISED — NOT YET ZIP-VERIFIED — the figure comes from a third-party or aggregator source and has not been confirmed as a quote available at a specific Rhode Island address. It is shown for the record and is never presented as a quote you can necessarily obtain.
  • PARTLY CONFIRMED — some fields (for example, the term and early-termination fee) were verified on the supplier’s own site while the rate itself remains an advertised figure. The card states exactly which is which.
  • NOT AVAILABLE IN RHODE ISLAND — the supplier was verified as not selling electricity in Rhode Island. Its advertised-elsewhere rate is excluded from the rankings and is never shown with a savings figure.

> HOW TRACK-RECORD FLAGS ARE ASSIGNED

Track-record flags come from one sourced dataset only: the Green Energy Consumers Alliance analysis of Rhode Island Division of Public Utilities and Carriers (DPUC) data for 2017–2024, which calculated, for each supplier, the average amount its Rhode Island customers paid compared with default service over that period, assuming use of 554 kWh per month. The same analysis found that third-party supply customers in Rhode Island paid $61.6 million more in total than they would have on default service between 2017 and 2024.

  • A supplier whose customers paid more on average is flagged with the exact average figure, the period (2017–2024), and the attribution, in the form: “customers paid +$X more on average vs default (2017–2024).”
  • Suppliers at or above a $900 average premium, and suppliers carrying a caution flag in the source data, are grouped separately below cleaner offers and are never eligible for a Top Honest Pick badge. The rule is mechanical — it is applied from the sourced figure, never by hand for an individual supplier.
  • A supplier whose customers saved money on average, or roughly broke even, is labeled that way with the same figure and period.
  • If no figure for a supplier exists in that dataset, the card reads “no RI track-record figure in our data.” We do not infer a track record from other states or other periods.
  • A track record is history, not a prediction. It describes an average over 2017–2024 at the stated usage assumption; it does not describe what any individual customer paid, or what a current offer will cost. Current-offer cost is shown separately, from the current rate, on the same card.

We describe track records in the sourced, factual terms above. We do not attach characterisations to a named supplier that the sourced data does not contain.

> HOW THE TOP HONEST PICK WORKS

The ★ TOP HONEST PICK badge is mechanical. An offer qualifies only if it meets all of these criteria at the same time:

  • a clean track record — no caution flag and no disqualifying average premium in the sourced 2017–2024 data described above;
  • a confirmed $0 early-termination fee;
  • a confirmed term;
  • a fixed rate; and
  • a rate that beats the Last Resort benchmark at the visitor’s stated usage.

Among qualifying offers, the badge goes to the qualifying offer with the lowest estimated supply cost at the stated usage. Payment never affects the badge, the ranking, or any flag — see our Affiliate Disclosure. If no offer qualifies, no badge is shown. The criteria line also appears on the card that carries the badge.

> HOW SAVINGS ARE CALCULATED

Estimated monthly supply cost = (rate in cents per kWh × monthly kWh ÷ 100) + any confirmed monthly fee. Savings compare supply charges only against the Last Resort benchmark at the same stated usage — the 600 kWh default, or the usage the visitor enters, labeled as such on the figure. Delivery charges do not change when you switch suppliers and are not part of the comparison. The state bill credits are shown as separate lines and are never counted as a supplier’s saving, because they are the same for every household. Where a monthly fee is not confirmed, the estimate excludes it and the card says so — the actual cost could be higher.

> CORRECTIONS

If you believe any figure on this site is wrong or out of date — a rate, a fee, a term, a track-record figure, a town rate — tell us:

  • Email: tomas.gregorio@gmail.com — include the supplier/offer, the figure you believe is wrong, and a source for the correct figure if you have one.
  • We will re-check the figure against its source. A challenged figure that cannot be promptly re-verified will be corrected or removed pending verification.
  • Corrections are logged. We keep a dated log of each correction request, what was checked, and what was changed, and material corrections are reflected in the data notes on the affected card.

Suppliers and towns may use the same address to request a correction to their own listing. A correction request never affects ranking or flags except through the verified data itself.
