> CORRECTIONS

If a rate, fee, term, track-record figure, or town rate shown here is wrong or out of date, tell us what you saw and where. Correction requests are checked against the primary source and, when confirmed, fixed and logged as described in our Methodology. A correction request never affects a ranking or a flag except through the verified data itself.

> SUPPLIERS AND TOWNS

Suppliers and municipal aggregation programs may use the same address to request a correction to their own listing or to submit a published offer for verification. Listings are free; there is no paid placement.

> PLEASE DO NOT SEND

Do not email your Rhode Island Energy account number, a full utility bill, payment details, or other personal documents — we never need them to answer a question about the rates shown here. This site has no accounts and cannot change, enroll, or cancel anyone's electricity service; for account service, contact Rhode Island Energy or your supplier directly.

RI RATE CHECK is an information-only comparison service. It is not affiliated with Rhode Island Energy, the RI Public Utilities Commission, the RI Division of Public Utilities and Carriers, Empower RI, or any listed supplier. Rates shown are estimates as of the stated checked date; verify all terms — rate, term, fees, and renewal behaviour — with the supplier before enrolling.
