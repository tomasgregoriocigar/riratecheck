== PRIVACY POLICY ==

Last updated: October 17, 2026. Operator: the Gregorio Companies digital LLC.

> NO ACCOUNTS

This site has no accounts, no sign-ups, and no enrollment. We do not ask for your name, address, email address, account number, or payment information in order to use the comparison.

> YOUR ZIP CODE AND USAGE INPUT

  • The ZIP code and monthly kWh usage you enter are used only in your own browser, only to display and calculate the rates shown to you.
  • They are not stored and not transmitted by this site to the operator or to any supplier. Leaving or reloading the page discards them.
  • Outbound links to a supplier’s site do not carry your ZIP code or usage with them from this site.

> WHAT YOUR BROWSER SENDS AUTOMATICALLY

Like any website, this site is delivered by a hosting provider, and your browser necessarily sends the host technical information needed to serve the page — such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — which the host may record in standard server logs. We do not collect any personal information beyond what your browser sends to the host in this way, and we do not use server logs to identify individual visitors or build profiles of them.

> ANALYTICS

Privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics may be used to understand how many people visit the site and which pages are useful (for example, total visits and popular pages). Any analytics used will be aggregate reporting about the audience as a whole — not advertising trackers, cross-site profiling, or fingerprinting. If the analytics approach changes materially, this policy will be updated first.

> NO SALE OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information with suppliers or advertisers for their own marketing. There is no lead-selling on this site: your visit is not a lead, and nothing about your visit is passed to a supplier by this site.

> EMAIL YOU SEND US

If you email us (for example, a correction request to tomas.gregorio@gmail.com), we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use them only to respond to you and, for corrections, to maintain the corrections log described in our Methodology. We do not add people who email us to any marketing list.

> THIRD-PARTY SITES AND CHANGES

Links on this site lead to supplier, agency, and town websites that this site does not control. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies after you arrive; please review them there. This policy may be updated as the site changes; the version posted here, with its update date, is the current version. Questions: tomas.gregorio@gmail.com.
