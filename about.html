== ABOUT RI RATE CHECK ==

Last updated: November 26, 2026. Operator: Gregorio Digital Companies LLC.

> WHAT THIS SITE IS

RI Rate Check is an independent comparison of Rhode Island electricity supply offers against the Rhode Island Energy Last Resort (default) rate. Every offer shown carries the source it was checked against and the date it was checked. Offers we cannot verify are labeled as advertised only, or left out. Suppliers whose Rhode Island customers have paid far more than the default on average — measured in RI Division of Public Utilities data — are flagged and listed apart, whatever their headline rate says.

> WHO RUNS IT

This site is operated by Gregorio Digital Companies LLC, a Rhode Island company. It is not affiliated with Rhode Island Energy, the RI Public Utilities Commission, the RI Division of Public Utilities and Carriers, Empower RI, or any listed supplier, and no supplier pays to be listed, ranked, or described here.

> HOW IT MAKES MONEY

The site is supported by advertising and, where disclosed, referral fees — only ever on options this site's own comparison ranks as genuine wins for the reader. A referral fee never changes whether an offer is shown, where it ranks, or what is said about it. See the Affiliate Disclosure for the current arrangements.

> HOW THE DATA IS KEPT HONEST

Rates come from primary sources — the Empower RI rate card, suppliers' own published pages and disclosures, RI PUC tariffs, and town program announcements — and are re-checked on a regular cycle and at every Last Resort reset (April 1 and October 1). The full method is on the Methodology page. If a figure here is wrong, we want to know: see Contact.

RI RATE CHECK is an information-only comparison service. It is not affiliated with Rhode Island Energy, the RI Public Utilities Commission, the RI Division of Public Utilities and Carriers, Empower RI, or any listed supplier. Rates shown are estimates as of the stated checked date; verify all terms — rate, term, fees, and renewal behaviour — with the supplier before enrolling.
